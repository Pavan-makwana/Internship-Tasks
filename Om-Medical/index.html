About Us

Founded in 2018, OM Medical was established with a vision to provide accessible, reliable, and high-quality healthcare to individuals and families in our community. Our experienced medical team delivers patient-centered care in a welcoming environment. Your health and trust are always our top priorities.

Our Services

Medicines

We provide a wide range of high-quality prescription and over-the-counter medicines sourced from trusted manufacturers. Our pharmacy team ensures safe dispensing, proper storage, and guidance on correct usage so you receive effective and reliable treatment.

Consultation

Our qualified healthcare professionals offer personalized medical consultations for diagnosis, treatment, and preventive care. We take time to understand your concerns and create tailored care plans to support your health and well-being.

Home Delivery

OM Medical offers convenient home delivery of medicines and healthcare products. Our fast and reliable delivery service ensures you receive your medications safely and on time, right at your doorstep.
